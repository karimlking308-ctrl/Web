Corporate Governance & Data Integrity

Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Compliance Standard: GDPR, CCPA & Global E-Commerce

Article 1.0

1. Introduction & Ethical Standard

At TinDry, our foundational ethos — "Wear Your Standard" — governs not only the 16+ years of technical precision engineered into our 320–480 GSM textiles, but also the ethical guardianship of your personal data.

We hold client confidentiality to the highest international benchmark. This Privacy Policy delineates with complete transparency how we collect, store, safeguard, and utilize information when you browse our boutique or purchase from our curated collections. We never monetize, rent, or trade customer data under any circumstance.

2. Information We Collect

Article 2.0

In delivering our high-end streetwear and bespoke unboxing experiences, we collect only the data essential to consummate orders and fulfill courier delivery:

▪ Personal Identifiers

Full legal name, billing address, discrete courier shipping destination, phone number (for courier dispatch notifications), and verified email address.

▪ Encrypted Payment Credentials

Card number, expiration, and CVV are tokenized and processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant financial gateways (Stripe, Apple Pay, Visa, Mastercard). TinDry never stores raw card details on internal servers.

▪ Technical Device Telemetry

IP address, browser type, geographic currency preference (USD, EUR, GBP), and referrer URLs used purely for site responsiveness and fraud prevention.

▪ Concierge Communications

Transcripts of sizing advisories, packaging inquiries, and bespoke tailoring requests communicated with our concierge specialists.

3. How We Use Your Information

Article 3.0

Your information is deployed strictly to execute our luxury commitment:

  • •
    Bespoke Fulfillment & Shipping: Hand-packing your garments in our rigid armor boxes, issuing courier waybills with DHL Express or FedEx, and broadcasting automated tracking links.
  • •
    Concierge Support & Fit Advisories: Providing rapid assistance regarding garment sanforization, sizing adjustments, and our 30-day exchange guarantee.
  • •
    Fraud Detection & Security Auditing: Screening suspicious transactions against global banking protocols to protect consumers from identity theft.
  • •
    Opt-In Capsule Releases: Transmitting private lookbook access and limited capsule drops only when explicitly requested by the customer.
Article 4.0

4. Rigorous Data Security Standards

Reflecting the exact technical discipline honed across 16+ years of manufacturing mastery, we enforce multiple layers of digital defenses:

256-Bit SSL/TLS

End-to-end cryptographic encryption across all checkout touchpoints.

PCI-DSS Tier 1

Certified payment gateway compliance matching global institutional banking.

Zero Data Sale

We strictly prohibit selling, trading, or licensing client information to data brokers.

All client databases operate within hardened, access-restricted cloud clusters subjected to regular vulnerability scans and automated anomaly detection.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

Article 5.0

Regardless of your jurisdiction, TinDry empowers every customer with total autonomy over their digital footprint:

You maintain the right to inspect, rectify, or request complete deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") of all personal records in our system. You may also opt out of promotional communications at any instant via the one-click unsubscribe mechanism located at the foot of all official dispatches.

Article 6.0

6. Privacy Inquiries & Legal Concierge

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your data rights, or request an audit of your order records, our dedicated privacy officers will attend to you promptly:

Direct Legal & Privacy Inquiries: privacy@tindry.com
Client Concierge Desk: concierge@tindry.com

Average legal inquiry response window: Under 24 business hours.
