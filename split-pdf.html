How to split a PDF?

Separating pages from a large PDF takes only a few seconds with Tindry. Everything runs locally on your computer or phone with zero uploads:

  1. 1. Upload your PDF — Drag and drop your PDF document into the drop zone above or click to select a file from your device.
  2. 2. Choose pages to extract — Pick Page Range (such as 1-3) or Specific Pages (such as 1,3,5), then click Split PDF.
  3. 3. Download your new PDF — Review the before and after file sizes and click Download PDF to save tindry-split.pdf instantly.

How Split PDF Works

Tindry Split PDF loads your document into local browser memory using client-side PDF parsing. Instead of uploading sensitive multi-page statements or contracts to external servers, your browser reads the PDF page tree directly on your CPU, copies only the page numbers you chose into a brand-new PDF container, and leaves all original fonts, links, and vector graphics intact. Once finished, you immediately see the original file size alongside your new smaller PDF before downloading.
