How to compress a PDF?

Making a PDF smaller for email attachments or upload limits takes just three steps in your browser:

  1. 1. Upload your PDF — Drag and drop your PDF document into the box above or click to pick a file from your phone or computer.
  2. 2. Choose compression level — Pick Low, Medium, or High depending on how much metadata cleanup and object compaction you want, then click Compress PDF.
  3. 3. Download your compressed PDF — Check the before and after sizes plus percentage saved, then click Download PDF to save tindry-compressed.pdf.

How Compress PDF Works

Many PDFs exported from desktop editors, scanners, or browsers carry unnecessary incremental save history, bloated cross-reference tables, orphaned objects, and personal metadata tags such as author names, titles, and keywords. Tindry Compress PDF rebuilds your file locally using compressed PDF object streams and strips hidden metadata fields without rasterizing pages or blurring text. If your PDF has already been tightly compressed beforehand, Tindry detects that and informs you transparently.

FAQ

How much can I compress?

Savings depend on how your PDF was originally exported. Documents with redundant revision history, uncompressed object tables, or heavy metadata often shrink noticeably, while already-compressed PDFs will show less than 5% reduction.

Will quality drop?

No. Because Tindry compacts PDF structure and strips metadata without degrading text or downsampling pages, your document keeps its sharp vector fonts and layout clarity.

Is it safe?

Yes. Your PDF never leaves your device. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser with zero server uploads and automatic metadata removal.
