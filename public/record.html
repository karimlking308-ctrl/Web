Long Recording Support

Tindry Record processes media in progressive 1-second chunks. On Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge), you can stream directly to your hard drive via the File System Access API to avoid browser RAM limits.

Actual maximum duration depends on your device performance, available disk space, and browser memory limits.

100% Private & Client-Side

Screen capture, optional audio mixing, and video encoding happen entirely inside your web browser sandbox. No video bytes or audio packets ever leave your device.
