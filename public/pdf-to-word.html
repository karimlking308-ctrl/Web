How to use?

Converting your PDF documents to editable Microsoft Word files on Tindry is smooth and straightforward. Drag and drop any PDF file into the designated upload area above, or click the box to browse documents from your Mac, Windows PC, iPhone, or Android device. Once selected, our built-in PDF parser immediately reads your file locally without transferring a single byte across the internet. You will see a live progress bar tracking each page extraction in real time. Once text extraction is finished, Tindry automatically packages the paragraphs, line formatting, and page structure into a standard .docx document. Click the black Download Word (.docx) button to save your editable document instantly.

What is this tool?

PDF to Word - Private is an in-browser document conversion utility engineered for speed and uncompromising confidential data protection. PDF (Portable Document Format) is ideal for preserving layout fidelity across different screens, but editing text within a compiled PDF is cumbersome and frustrating. Tindry's converter extracts text streams, structural lines, and page breaks from your PDF and dynamically reconstructs them into an OpenXML Microsoft Word document (.docx). The resulting file can be opened, styled, edited, and revised in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Apple Pages, or LibreOffice Writer without requiring Adobe Acrobat subscriptions.

Privacy & Security

Business contracts, employee records, tax filings, financial spreadsheets, and academic dissertations contain highly confidential intellectual property and personal information. Uploading these sensitive files to remote web servers introduces grave data leak and regulatory compliance liabilities. Tindry guarantees ironclad confidentiality: every parsing and document generation routine is performed inside your local browser memory sandbox. Your original PDF files and generated Word documents never touch a remote server, external database, or third-party API. When you close the browser tab, all memory buffers are instantly purged.

FAQ

Does this tool work on scanned PDFs?

This tool extracts live text streams directly embedded within digital PDFs. If your PDF is an image scan without an embedded text layer, consider running OCR first.

Will the converted Word document preserve page breaks?

Yes. The converter respects individual page divisions and adds standard page breaks in your generated Word document so page boundaries remain intact.

Is there a file size limit or page cap?

There are no artificial caps. Since processing happens locally on your computer or mobile device, performance depends entirely on your available device memory.

Are my documents uploaded to any server?

Never. Tindry is 100% client-side. Your documents stay strictly within your browser and never leave your phone or computer.
