Frequently Asked Questions

How does SolMarket escrow work?

When you buy a project, your SOL is deposited into an audited Solana smart contract escrow vault. The seller transfers the code/repository, and once you verify and confirm, funds are instantly released to the seller.

Why Solana only?

Solana provides sub-second finality and near-zero transaction fees, ensuring seamless escrow deposits, arbitration, and payouts without losing value to gas fees.

What happens if there is a dispute?

If the delivered project does not match the description, you can open a dispute. Our decentralized arbiters review cryptographic proof and code repositories to issue a fair refund or release.
