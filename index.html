Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers on our heavy-gauge fabric GSM, care and washing methodologies, signature packaging, and expedited global dispatch.

GSM stands for Grams per Square Meter and denotes the physical density of the knit. While conventional commercial t-shirts average a flimsy 140–180 GSM, TinDry garments are engineered at an ultra-heavyweight 320 GSM (T-Shirts) and 480 GSM (Hoodies).

This provides a structured, architectural drape that falls straight over the torso rather than clinging. It provides substantial substance, total opacity (zero sheer transparency), and extreme durability that gets softer with every wash while retaining its original shape.

All our cotton is pre-treated with proprietary 2-step sanforization, making shrinkage negligible under proper care:

  • Machine Wash Cold: 30°C (85°F) on a gentle cycle inside-out to preserve the deep obsidian pigment.
  • Line Dry Flat: Always hang dry or flat dry in the shade. Heavyweight cotton absorbs more moisture, and tumble drying on high heat may stress natural cotton fibers.
  • Zero Bleach / Neutral Detergent: Avoid harsh optical brighteners or synthetic fabric softeners.
  • Collar Care: Our twin-needle collar ribbing is engineered with integrated elastic thread memory — it will never buckle or "bacon" over time.
